# Waggie Tail

Waggie Tail is the social network where the dog is the account.

The platform combines:

- Family dog social networking.
- Shelter and rescue advocacy.
- Lifelong dog identity.
- Adoption handoff.
- Campaigns and contests.
- Agent-assisted profile operations.
- National shelter coverage.
- Marketing and impact analytics.

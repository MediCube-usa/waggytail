# Working Session: Premium Social Benchmark Refresh

Date: 2026-08-16
Milestone: Milestone C — Premium Visual Social Core

## Objective
Elevate the Waggie Tail preview so it feels like a premium human-quality social network where dogs are the public accounts and real dog photography carries the brand.

## Research Notes
- High-quality social platforms use strong visual hierarchy, consistent interaction patterns, accessible contrast, premium spacing, and recognizable feed/profile/discovery loops.
- Instagram-style products rely on polished real-photo media, profile credibility, stories, engagement actions, and mobile-first navigation.
- TikTok-style discovery emphasizes momentum, creator identity, and quick content scanning.
- Pinterest-style surfaces emphasize aspirational image grids and saved inspiration.
- Professional social products use trust signals, clear status labels, and restrained system controls.

## Work Completed
- Reframed the hero around “dogs run the world” and premium social benchmarking.
- Replaced the emoji-style logo mark with a restrained monogram treatment.
- Added benchmark chips that clarify the comparison target: Instagram-grade photo feed, TikTok-speed discovery, Pinterest-level polish, and LinkedIn-level trust systems.
- Strengthened the visual system with editorial typography, premium background structure, glass surfaces, deeper shadows, and more polished cards.
- Preserved real dog photos from Unsplash and avoided AI-dog imagery.
- Updated shelter and campaign copy to keep the premium social tone while preserving evidence and adoption trust language.

Run tests, linting, type checking, and production build.

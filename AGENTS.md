# Waggie Tail Agent Operating System

## Prime Directive

Build and operate Waggie Tail as a complete integrated platform where every dog has a separate public identity and authorized people or organizations manage those identities through secure private accounts.

GitHub is the permanent source of truth for architecture, decisions, code, tests, operations, and recovery.

## Product North Star

> Waggie Tail is the social network where the dog is the account.

Mission:

> Every dog deserves a profile, a voice, an audience, and a path home.

The product serves:

1. Dogs living with families.
2. Dogs waiting for families.

## Locked Architecture

1. Every dog has a separate public account.
2. Human and organization logins remain private management identities.
3. One person or organization may manage many dog accounts.
4. One dog may have several authorized managers.
5. Family dogs and shelter dogs share the same social network.
6. The Shelter Voice Engine is a core platform system.
7. Each shelter fact retains source provenance and freshness data.
8. Sensitive claims require exact approved source evidence.
9. A missing source record maps to Status Unknown and verification.
10. Adoption status requires authorized evidence.
11. A dog's identity can transfer to an adopter after confirmed adoption.
12. Campaigns operate through a reusable configuration engine.
13. The son operates the platform through plain-language interface controls.
14. AI prepares drafts and controlled jobs inside permission, source, and approval rules.
15. GitHub records the final architecture and decisions.

## Evidence and Publication Rules

Public shelter profiles require:

- Registered organization.
- Active source policy.
- Stable dog identifier.
- Current availability status.
- Official adoption or contact path.
- Media-rights record or approved placeholder.
- Safe general location.
- Duplicate clearance.
- Field provenance.
- Freshness timestamp.
- Correction and removal workflow.

Health, behavior, safety, compatibility, urgency, adoption, and location claims require explicit approved source fields.

# Waggie Tail North Star

Status: LOCKED
Date: 2026-08-15

## Product

Waggie Tail is the social network where every dog can have a distinct identity, voice, audience, and continuing story.

## Positioning

> The social network where the dog is the account.

## Mission

> Every dog deserves a profile, a voice, an audience, and a path home.

## Populations Served

1. Dogs living with families.
2. Dogs waiting for families.

## Public Identity

The dog is the public identity.

Humans, shelters, rescues, fosters, advocates, and administrators operate behind the dog profile through authorized management relationships.

## Core Product Pillars

### Family Dog Social Network

Dogs post, follow, comment, enter campaigns, share stories, and build audiences.

### Shelter Voice Network

Dogs waiting for homes receive social identities, first-person stories, followers, repeated visibility, and official adoption pathways.

### Lifelong Dog Identity

A dog's profile can move from shelter management to adopter management while preserving history, followers, posts, and community.

### Agent-Assisted Operations

Agents help discover, normalize, draft, maintain, promote, and measure dog profiles through source, permission, review, and audit controls.

## Operating Goal

Build a national dog social network that combines entertainment, community, rescue advocacy, adoption visibility, and lifelong identity in one integrated platform.
